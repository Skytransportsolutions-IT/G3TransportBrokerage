# Media credits & licensing

All photography and video currently on the site is **placeholder stock** sourced from
[Pexels](https://www.pexels.com). None of it shows G3 Transport Inc facilities or people.

**G3 is a freight brokerage, not an asset carrier.** No image here should be presented as G3-owned
equipment. Photos of trucks are legitimate as illustrations of the freight G3 moves; captioning one
as "our truck" would not be.

Not permitted under that license, and not done here: reselling the images unaltered, or implying the
identifiable people in them endorse a product or service. **This matters for the driver portrait** —
if it stays on the site past the blueprint stage, it risks reading as a G3 employee endorsement.
Replace it with a real G3 driver (with their written permission) before launch.

## Clips considered and rejected

- An aerial of a truck stop full of trailers — rejected: prominent third-party branding (TA, Michelin)
  and it reads as a facility G3 does not own.
- A semi passing camera on a commercial strip — rejected: Shell and Burger King signage dominate the frame.

The same test should be applied to any replacement: **no other company's branding should appear in a
photo used to represent G3.**
