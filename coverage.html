Where we load

California growing regions

Our carrier network works the origins that matter. If your cooler or packing shed sits in one of these regions, we already have trucks running past your door.

Salinas Valley & Central Coast

Salinas, Watsonville, Gilroy, Santa Maria, Oxnard. Leafy greens, strawberries, celery, broccoli and cool-season vegetables.

San Joaquin Valley

Bakersfield, Fresno, Visalia, Stockton, Modesto. Grapes, citrus, stone fruit, carrots, melons, almonds and tomatoes.

Imperial & Coachella Valley

El Centro, Brawley, Indio. Winter lettuce and greens, melons, dates and mixed vegetables through the desert season.

Sacramento Valley

Sacramento, Yuba City, Woodland. Tomatoes, rice, walnuts, prunes and row crops.

Southern California & ports

Los Angeles, Vernon, San Diego. Wholesale markets, import produce and cross-dock facilities.
