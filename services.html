Services

Refrigerated freight brokerage built around perishable produce — from a single truckload of strawberries to a standing weekly program.

Core service

Refrigerated truckload

53' reefer capacity sourced from our carrier network, California growing regions to receivers nationwide. One truck, one load, no rehandling between pickup and delivery.

  • Pre-cool required in writing. The trailer is at set point before it reaches the shipper, and the rate confirmation says so.
  • Continuous run unless you specify otherwise, with the reefer download available on request after delivery.
  • Sealed loads. Seal number recorded on the bill of lading at origin and checked at delivery.
  • Food-grade carriers only. Trailer inspection and wash-out are part of what we require before dispatch.

Consolidation

Multi-stop & distribution loads

Not every shipper fills a trailer. We build loads across neighboring growing regions and route them into multiple receivers on the same run, keeping each commodity at a compatible temperature.

  • Compatibility checked before we combine commodities on one trailer.
  • Stop sequence planned around receiver hours and unload times.
  • Separate paperwork and POD per stop.
Return capacity

Backhauls into California

The carriers we load out of California need freight going home. If you ship into the state — produce, packaging, dry goods or general freight — we can match it to a truck already heading west and price it accordingly.
