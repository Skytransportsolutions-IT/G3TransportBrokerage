Why shippers stay with us

A broker that understands perishables

Produce is not general freight. A late arrival or a drifting set point is not a service failure — it is a rejected load. We book every load with that in mind, and we do not hand your freight to a carrier we have not checked.

  • Temperature discipline. Pre-cool and set-point requirements go on the rate confirmation, and we can pull the reefer download for any load.
  • Appointment reality. We plan around cooler hours and receiver windows, not optimistic map times.
  • Real updates. We track the truck and call you — you should not be phoning us to find out where your load is.
  • Carrier vetting on every load. Authority, insurance, safety history and fraud watch-lists checked before a truck is dispatched.

Leafy greens lose shelf life by the hour. We line the truck up before the load is ready, hold the carrier to the set point, and keep it moving — because every hour saved at origin is an hour your receiver gets back at the far end.
