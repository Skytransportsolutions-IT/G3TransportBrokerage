03 — Photography & video

Every photo here is a placeholder

The site now carries real photography and two background video clips so you can judge the design as a finished piece. None of it shows G3 equipment.

Important: these are licensed stock photos and clips (Pexels License — free for commercial use, no attribution required). They show other companies’ trucks and other growers’ fields. Because G3 is a brokerage rather than an asset carrier, no photo here should be captioned as “our truck” — the honest framing is freight G3 moved, or carriers G3 works with. Ask your best carriers for permission to use photos of their equipment on your loads, and shoot your own office and team.

What is in place

  • 23 photographs, each cropped and sized for its slot (3.5 MB total)
  • Hero background video — highway, 12 seconds, 1.8 MB
  • Field background video — leafy greens, 9 seconds, 0.7 MB
  • Poster frames so every video section looks right before it plays
  • Videos are muted, loop, and pause when scrolled off screen
  • Motion is disabled entirely for visitors who ask for reduced motion

The shot list we would ask a photographer for

  • Your office and the freight desk — the team actually working
  • Headshots of the reps a shipper will speak to
  • A carrier’s truck on one of your loads, with their permission
  • Loading at a cooler or packing house you regularly work with
  • Reefer unit and set-point display, close up
  • Loaded pallets of the commodities you actually move
  • The growing regions you cover, in season
04 — Open items

What we need from you

Everything highlighted in yellow across the site is a placeholder. Here is the full list, grouped so you can answer it in one sitting.

Contact & legal

  • Phone number(s) — dispatch, recruiting, accounting
  • Email addresses to publish
  • Office street address, city, ZIP
  • Office hours vs dispatch hours
  • Broker MC number and USDOT number
  • Domain name to launch on

Operations

  • Carrier network size — approved carriers
  • Temperature range and max payload you source
  • Real transit times per region
  • Which growing regions you actually load
  • Whether you cover Yuma / border crossings
  • Whether to promote dry-freight backhauls

Commercial

  • Accessorial charges — keep the table or drop it
  • Shipper payment terms and credit process
  • BMC-84 surety bond amount and provider
  • Contingent cargo and general liability limits
  • Carrier vetting tools you use (RMIS, Highway, Carrier411…)
  • Year founded

Carrier terms

  • Quick pay percentage and timing
  • Standard payment terms (net days)
  • Carrier insurance minimums — auto liability and cargo
  • Whether reefer breakdown coverage is required
  • Detention and layover policy paid to carriers
  • Loads moved per month, and carriers in the network

Content & photos

  • Founding story — who, when, why
  • Real customer testimonials with permission
  • Photos: trucks, trailers, loading, team, office — to replace the stock placeholders
  • Team names, titles and bios (or drop the section)

Technical

  • Where form submissions should be delivered
  • Hosting preference
  • Google Business Profile / analytics accounts
  • Spanish-language version — wanted or not?
  • Privacy policy and terms content
